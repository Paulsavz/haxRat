Place your app icons here before building:

  icon.png   — 512×512 PNG  (used by Linux AppImage & as fallback)
  icon.ico   — Windows multi-size ICO  (used by NSIS installer)
  icon.icns  — macOS ICNS bundle       (used by DMG)

You can generate icon.ico and icon.icns from icon.png using tools such as:
  • https://convertio.co/png-ico/
  • https://www.img2icnsapp.com/  (macOS)
  • electron-icon-builder  (npm package)

Until real icons are added the app will still run but the taskbar/tray
icon will appear blank on some platforms.
